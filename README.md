# Eng Hoe Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Eng Hoe Pte Ltd supplies power protection equipment and provides engineering integration and technical support for critical applications in Singapore. The proposed homepage helps technical buyers, facilities teams, and data centre operators find equipment and request support.

## Main Improvements

1. **Lead with critical power protection.** The existing homepage opens with a broad list of products and services. A more specific headline would explain how Eng Hoe supports industrial facilities and data centres through power protection equipment, integration, and technical support.
2. **Organise products around four solution areas.** The existing navigation spreads related equipment across several categories. The proposed homepage would group products into UPS and industrial batteries, electrical infrastructure, precision cooling, and integrated data centre solutions, with a direct link to each product family.
3. **Bring technical services onto the homepage.** Preventive maintenance, commissioning, training, battery replacement, and troubleshooting currently sit under Support. A dedicated section would make these services easier to find and give visitors a direct route to a technical enquiry.

## Design Improvements

A focused opening section would establish the engineering proposition. Consistently structured product cards and a dedicated technical services section would give visitors clear entry points for equipment and support.
